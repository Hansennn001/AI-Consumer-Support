COMPANY REFUND POLICY

1. Refund Request

Customer can request a refund within 14 days after purchase.

2. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days.

3. Refund Eligibility

Refund is not available for products that have been fully consumed.

4. Payment Issue

If payment is deducted but service is unavailable, customer should contact support.
